How does a SIP work?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows an investor to invest a fixed amount regularly, usually every month. Over time, the investment may grow through the effect of compounding.

Important: These calculators provide estimates based on the return assumptions entered. Actual investment returns can be higher or lower and are not guaranteed.

SIP Calculator — Frequently Asked Questions

What is SIP?

SIP stands for Systematic Investment Plan. It is a method of investing a fixed amount at regular intervals.

What is a Step-up SIP?

A Step-up SIP increases your monthly investment periodically, commonly by a fixed percentage every year.

Is SIP return guaranteed?

No. The return used in these calculators is only an assumption for estimation. Market-linked investments can fluctuate.

What does estimated future value mean?

It is the estimated value of your investment at the end of the selected period based on the return assumption entered.

Can I change the SIP amount?

Yes. Enter any monthly investment amount and calculate again.
